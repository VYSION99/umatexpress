# Hostel Finder — public information

## Short description

**Hostel Finder helps UMaT students find and compare approved hostels near campus.** Browse without an account, see available bed spaces and yearly prices, request a visit, and book eligible beds online.

## How it works

1. Choose an academic year and compare hostels by location, price, available beds, and utilities.
2. Open a property to review its photos, individual bed prices, staff-check records, reviews, and travel estimates.
3. Request an in-person viewing or ask the hostel a question before deciding.
4. Sign in with your UMaT student account to hold an available bed and pay through the checkout.
5. After payment is confirmed, use the resident area to view your booking and contact the hostel.

## Prices and room layout

Prices are shown per student bed for the selected academic year. Each bed in one room has the same yearly rent for that year. A bunk has an upper bed and a lower bed; each is a separate place and booking. Where utilities are enabled, the listing shows the utilities amount and total for that bed.

A booking holds one bed. To reserve multiple beds, each bed must be booked separately and must still be available.

## Trust and travel information

Only approved bed listings appear in public search. Photos and floor plans are reviewed before publication. A “What staff checked” card shows dated review records for supported details. A past review is not a guarantee that details have not changed; contact the hostel to confirm anything important.

The distance on search cards is an approximate straight-line distance. If routing data is available, the property page separately shows an estimated walking route to a selected campus destination. Map pins, distances, and route times are estimates.

## Visits and questions

A viewing request does not reserve a bed. The hostel must confirm the requested time. The property assistant answers using the public listing and information supplied by the hostel. Students can send a follow-up to staff when they need a specific answer.

## Booking and refunds

A student account is required to book. Selecting a bed starts a ten-minute payment hold while checkout is completed. A bed is confirmed only after the payment is verified. Some hostels may be visible while booking is temporarily paused for payout review.

Refund requests are reviewed by staff. Current policy quotes a full refund at least 30 days before the academic year starts, half between 7 and 29 days, and no refund inside the final 7 days. An administrator may approve a policy exception with a recorded reason. An approved refund is complete only after the payment has settled.

## Important note

Availability, prices, viewing times, and property details can change. Recheck the selected bed immediately before payment, read the price breakdown, and contact the hostel if any detail is unclear. Hostel Finder publishes reviewed information; it does not replace a student’s own visit or confirmation.
